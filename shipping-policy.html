Shipping Policy — AKARRA

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 working days after payment confirmation. Processing may take longer during holidays, sales or periods of high order volume.

2. Shipping and Delivery Timeline

Estimated delivery time is 3–7 working days after dispatch. Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary depending on the delivery location, courier availability, weather or other circumstances beyond our control.

3. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be displayed at checkout or communicated before the order is confirmed.

4. Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking details will be shared with the customer after dispatch.

5. Delivery Address and Contact Details

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number. Delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect details may require additional coordination or shipping arrangements.

6. Damaged or Incorrect Deliveries

If an order arrives damaged or you receive an incorrect product, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. Include your order details, clear product photographs and a complete, continuous and unedited unboxing video recorded from the time you receive/pick up the sealed parcel, clearly showing the sealed package and the entire opening process. The video is mandatory for the standard replacement review, without limiting applicable consumer rights.

Replacement eligibility and the process are explained in our Replacement Policy.
