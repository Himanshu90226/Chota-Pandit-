Replacement Policy — AKARRA

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Replacement and Refund Eligibility

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact AKARRA support within 24 hours of delivery. After reviewing the order details and evidence, AKARRA may offer an eligible customer a replacement or refund. The available resolution will be confirmed by our support team.

2. Mandatory Unboxing Video

A complete, continuous and unedited unboxing video is mandatory for the standard replacement review. Start recording when you receive or pick up the sealed parcel, show the sealed package clearly, and continue recording throughout the entire opening process without cuts or pauses.

3. How to Request a Replacement

Within 24 hours of delivery, contact support with your order details, clear photos of the product and parcel, and the complete unboxing video. Please contact support before sending any item anywhere. Our team will review the information and explain the next steps for an eligible replacement.

Refunds, where approved, will be processed to the original payment method where feasible, or through another mutually agreed lawful method. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank.

This policy does not limit any rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable consumer law.
